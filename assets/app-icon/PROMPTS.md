# Icon generation notes

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. The Light artwork was approved by the user. Dark and Glass were generated as edits using Light as the composition reference. Resizing for PNG / Xcode exports uses `Export-AppIcons.ps1`; it does not regenerate artwork.

## Light — generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app icon for an original macOS photo-wall application called Dynamic Photo Wall, also usable for its Windows version. Create one exquisitely polished, minimal, Apple-platform-inspired app icon, 1024x1024 square composition. The symbol is a horizontal gallery of THREE large upright rounded photographic panels, gently overlapping with the middle panel slightly forward, all nearly front-facing. Each pane contains a very simplified flowing landscape made of just a few broad smooth gradients: left sapphire and sky blue with one tiny white sun, middle mint and teal, right apricot and warm coral. The three panels express photos flowing across multiple screens. Use a refined translucent frosted-glass material feeling, restrained soft luminous edges, optically balanced shapes, and subtle depth without ornate bevels or dramatic 3D perspective. A quiet pale icy-white background with an extremely subtle cool-blue tint fills the ENTIRE square edge to edge. Center the three-pane motif and make it occupy about 76 percent of the width and 65 percent of the height. Broad filled shapes and clean silhouettes, legible at 16px. This must look like a considered native Mac application icon, not a website illustration or a photoreal product mockup. No letters, no text, no labels, no Apple logo, no Photos flower symbol, no camera lens, no extra objects, no decorative sparkles. Do not draw an outer rounded-square tile or any external drop shadow: output full-bleed square artwork so the operating system can apply its own icon mask. No frame, no blank margin around the canvas, no perspective scene.

## Dark — edit direction (condensed)

Use case: style-transfer. Create the Dark appearance variant of the approved Light icon. Preserve the three-panel composition, order, size, sun, and landscape shapes. Replace the pale backdrop with full-bleed inky navy / slate. Keep the blue, mint / teal, and apricot / coral panels, with luminous tinted glass edges against the dark backdrop. No text, outer mask, repositioning, or additional objects.

## Glass — final edit prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Create the polished LIQUID GLASS APPEARANCE variant of this approved app icon. Keep exactly the same full square composition, three rounded overlapping upright photo panels, left sun and flowing landscapes, sizes and placement. Transform the colorful panels into luminous clear silver crystal glass, white highlights and pearl-gray landscape ribbons, translucent refractive smooth surfaces. Soft cool silver-gray full-bleed opaque background, gently lit from top left. Clean smooth precision rounded silhouettes with subtle optical depth and restrained glass rims, no noise, no particles, no sparkles. The glass panels should be unmistakable against the backdrop with gentle soft shadows. This is a finished 1024 square app icon artwork with no outer rounded-square mask and no text. Opaque background fills the entire square, no cutout, no outside frame.

Transparent cutout experiments were rejected because the generated alpha had visible fringe artifacts. Only the approved composition and the three finished opaque artworks are shipped. Native Icon Composer layers remain a separate Mac-side design step; see [README](README.md).
